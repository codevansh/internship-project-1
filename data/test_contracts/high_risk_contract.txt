HIGH-RISK COMMERCIAL AGREEMENT

ARTICLE 1 - FINANCIAL COMMITMENTS

1.1 Minimum Purchase Commitment
The Customer is committed to purchase a minimum amount of $500,000 of products during each contract year.

1.2 Payment Obligation
All amounts owed by the Customer shall be immediately due upon demand. The Customer shall remain responsible for all unpaid amounts without limitation.

ARTICLE 2 - LIABILITY

2.1 Unlimited Liability
The Customer shall have unlimited liability for all losses, damages, claims, costs, expenses, and liabilities arising from or relating to this Agreement.

2.2 No Liability Cap
The Customer's liability shall not exceed no maximum amount and shall not be subject to any limitation.

2.3 Damages
The Customer shall pay liquidated damages of $250,000 for each material breach of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 3 - TERMINATION

3.1 Immediate Termination
The Service Provider may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice upon any breach by the Customer.

3.2 Termination Payment
Upon termination, the Customer shall immediately pay all remaining minimum commitments for the entire five year term.

ARTICLE 4 - COMPETITION

4.1 Non-Compete
The Customer shall not compete with the Service Provider or provide competing services during the Agreement and for three years following termination.

ARTICLE 5 - ASSIGNMENT

5.1 Assignment
The Customer shall not assign, transfer, delegate, or otherwise dispose of any rights or obligations under this Agreement without the Service Provider's approval.

ARTICLE 6 - LONG-TERM COMMITMENT

6.1 Contract Term
This Agreement shall remain in effect for five years and the Customer shall remain committed to the minimum purchase requirements throughout the entire term.

ARTICLE 7 - GOVERNING LAW

7.1 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws selected exclusively by the Service Provider.
