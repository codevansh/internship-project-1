SERVICE AGREEMENT

ARTICLE 1 - SERVICES

1.1 Services
The Service Provider shall provide software development and maintenance services to the Client as described in the applicable project statement.

1.2 Service Standards
The Service Provider shall perform the services in a professional and reasonable manner and shall comply with the agreed project requirements.

ARTICLE 2 - PAYMENT

2.1 Fees
The Client shall pay the Service Provider a fixed fee of $10,000 for the services described in this Agreement.

2.2 Payment Terms
Invoices shall be payable within 30 days of receipt. No additional charges shall apply unless approved in writing by both parties.

ARTICLE 3 - CONFIDENTIALITY

3.1 Confidential Information
Each party shall protect confidential information received from the other party and shall use such information only for purposes of performing this Agreement.

ARTICLE 4 - TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 Term
This Agreement shall commence on January 1, 2027 and shall remain in effect for one year.

4.2 Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing 30 days written notice to the other party.

ARTICLE 5 - LIABILITY

5.1 Limitation of Liability
Except for obligations that cannot legally be limited, each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid under this Agreement.

ARTICLE 6 - GOVERNING LAW

6.1 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

ARTICLE 7 - GENERAL

7.1 Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

7.2 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter described herein.
